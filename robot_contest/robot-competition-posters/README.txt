STEAM Robot Challenge - editable posters
========================================

Six A4 posters at 300 DPI (2480 x 3508 px), one per logo concept.

  01-build-a-robot.psd           Main announcement (Brick Bot logo)
  02-how-it-works.psd            The three steps of the competition (STEAM Gears logo)
  03-what-the-judges-ask.psd     The five STEAM question areas (Champion Seal logo)
  04-every-builder-belongs.psd   All ages, kindergarten to senior middle school (Growing Builders logo)
  05-design-build-explain.psd    For older students (Blueprint logo)
  06-make-it-beautiful.psd       Art counts too (Art Bot logo)

previews/ holds a flat PNG of each poster.

BEFORE YOU OPEN THE PSD FILES
1. Install every font in the fonts/ folder (double-click each .ttf, then Install).
   They are free Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License.
2. Open a PSD in Photoshop. It will say some text layers need updating.
   Click "Update". Photoshop then redraws the text with the installed fonts.

LAYERS IN EACH FILE
  Text (editable)   every word on the poster, one text layer each
  Artwork           logo, illustrations, panels and decorations, one layer each
  Background colour a single flat colour layer

THINGS TO FILL IN
  [DATE], [VENUE] and [HOW TO REGISTER] are blanks for your event details.
  "STEAM Robot Challenge" is a working name. Change it in the text layers.
  The name printed on the round seal in poster 03 is part of the artwork, not text.
  The questions on poster 03 are examples. Replace them with your judges' real questions.
